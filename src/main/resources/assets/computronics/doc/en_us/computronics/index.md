# Computronics: Rebooted

Welcome to **Computronics: Rebooted**, an expansion mod for **OpenComputers** on Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge. This addon extends your computer systems with industrial audio recording, advanced telecommunications, atmospheric sensors, security hardware, robotics peripherals, modular control surfaces, and server rack boards.

## Blocks & Components

- [Tape Drive](block/tape_drive.md): Read, write, and play magnetic audio tapes with seek support.
- [Chat Box](block/chat_box.md): Broadcast and whisper in global or localized Minecraft chat.
- [Speech Box](block/speech_box.md): Text-to-speech voice synthesizer.
- [Radar](block/radar.md): Scan for players and entities in a 3D spherical radius.
- [Camera](block/camera.md): Raycast distance measurement and surface block analysis.
- [Acoustic Sensor](block/acoustic_sensor.md): Sculk-based vibration and noise detector.
- [RF Jammer](block/rf_jammer.md): Disrupt and scramble wireless radio communication on chosen frequencies.
- [Weather Station](block/weather_station.md): Monitor atmospheric pressure, light, storms, and forecasts.
- [Inductive Charger](block/inductive_charger.md): Wirelessly charge drones and robots resting above the plate.
- [Laser Transceiver](block/laser_transceiver.md): Point-to-point optical data bridging up to 64 blocks with optical tripwire detection.
- [Analog Gauge](block/analog_gauge.md): Programmable physical dial meter for monitoring power, fuel, or pressure.
- [Modular Control Panel](block/control_panel.md): 8x8 physical dashboard grid holding miniature lamps and tactile push buttons.
- [Entity Detector](block/entity_detector.md): Proximity sensor measuring distance and coordinates of nearby living beings.
- [Iron Note Block](block/iron_noteblock.md): High-fidelity polyphonic musical MIDI note synthesizer.

## Modular Control Panel Elements & Portable Hardware

- [Miniature Indicator Lamp](item/mini_lamp.md): Compact surface-mountable 24-bit RGB LED lamp.
- [Miniature Push Button](item/mini_button.md): Tactile push button for control panels and wall interfaces.
- [Wireless VRAM Pager](item/pocket_terminal.md): Portable handheld monochrome & color matrix display receiving wireless telemetry.

## Server Rack Mountable Boards

- [Server Rack Light Board](item/rack_board_light.md): 4-LED status indicator board with animation modes.
- [Server Rack Switch Board](item/rack_board_switch.md): 4 physical toggle switches interactable from rack face.
- [Server Rack Detonator Board](item/rack_board_boom.md): Emergency rack self-destruct board with countdown.
- [Server Rack Capacitor Board](item/rack_board_capacitor.md): 50,000 EU rack UPS power buffer.
- [Server Rack Cryptographic Co-Processor](item/rack_board_co_processor.md): High-speed hardware ASIC for MD5, SHA-256, CRC32, and AES-128.
- [Server Rack KVM Switch Module](item/rack_board_kvm.md): 4-port console routing module switchable from rack face.

## Expansion Cards & Upgrades

- [Tier 3 Network Card](item/network_card_3.md): High-throughput network card supporting 64 KB Jumbo Frames, 64 open ports, and MAC/source address spoofing.
- [Packet Sniffer](item/packet_sniffer.md): Promiscuous TAP card to mirror network packets, analyze protocol traffic, and measure bandwidth.
- [RF Signal Jammer Card](item/rf_jammer_card.md): Mobile radio transmitter card to disrupt and scramble wireless communication on the move.
- [Self-Destruct Card](item/card_boom.md): Timed or immediate thermal detonation card.
- [Beep Card](item/card_beep.md): Programmable tone generator card.
- [Drone Winch Upgrade](item/winch_upgrade.md): Deployable mechanical cable and winch hook for drones.
- [Speech Upgrade](item/speech_upgrade.md): Voice synthesis card for mobile robots and drones.
- [Chat Upgrade](item/chat_upgrade.md): Allows robots and drones to speak and whisper.
- [Radar Upgrade](item/radar_upgrade.md): Equips mobile robots with active radar tracking.
- [Camera Upgrade](item/camera_upgrade.md): Gives drones and robots optical distance sensors.
- [Magnetic Tapes](item/tape.md): High-density storage media ranging from 128 KB to 1 MB.
