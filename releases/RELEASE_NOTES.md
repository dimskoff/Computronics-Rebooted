## 🌟 What's New in Computronics: Rebooted (v2.0.0-dev.1)

### 🎛️ Modular Control Panel System
- **Ultra-thin Surface Mounting (2 voxels):** Mounts flush on Floor, Ceiling, or Walls (North, South, East, West).
- **8×8 Grid (64 Slots):** Mount buttons, lamps, switches, 7-segment displays, and text labels.
- **Slanted 7-Segment Displays:** MicroTechnics-accurate proportions with trapezoid segments, italic slant (~6.3°), acrylic traces for unlit segments, and multi-digit formats (`1x1`, `1x2`, `1x3`, `1x4`).
- **Tactile Push Buttons:** Physical 10-tick depression with mechanical click audio and 3D in-world custom text.
- **Indicator RGB Lamps:** Sleek flat lens with core glow and 3D text labels.
- **Industrial Rocker Switches:** Dual-state physical toggle with glowing status LED.
- **Pure Text Labels (`ItemPanelText`):** Floating 3D text in sizes `1x1`, `2x1`, `4x1`, `8x1`, `2x2`, `4x2`.
- **Screwdriver Tool (`ItemScrewdriver`):** Left-click dismantles components safely; Right-click opens spacious dark-industrial 350×240 GUI.

### 📼 Digital Tape Drive
- **8-Bit DFN Audio Streaming:** High-fidelity cassette audio streaming into Minecraft OpenAL sound engine.
- **Animated Cassette Slot:** Inserting a cassette physically slides it deep into the chassis.
- **Tiered Cassettes:** Iron (4 min), Gold (8 min), Diamond (32 min), Nether Star (128 min).

### 📟 Wireless Pocket Terminal
- **Ergonomic One-Handed First-Person Hold:** Held like a map in a boat in the right hand without re-equip jitter.
- **32×12 Green Phosphor Matrix CRT:** Embedded wireless receiver listens on modem ports.
- **Inductive Charging:** Wirelessly charges over Inductive Charger Pads.

### 🗣️ Clean Text-to-Speech Engine
- **Speech Box Block & Robot Upgrade:** Clear narrator TTS voice in Russian and English with all legacy bit-beeps removed.

### 🖥️ Server Rack Infrastructure
- **Light Board, Switch Board, Rack Capacitor, Cryptographic Co-Processor, and Rack KVM Switch Module**.
